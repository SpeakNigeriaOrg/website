Online courses for children & teens

Joyful, play-based Yorùbá.

We teach Yorùbá to children and teens ages 5–17. Our focus is foundation-building — a confident, joyful start in the language — with classes grouped by age and level so every learner is with peers at the right stage.

How we teach

Learning that feels like play.

Play-based & joyful

Children learn through games, songs, stories, and conversation. When it's fun, they stay curious — and they keep coming back.

Foundation-building

We focus on strong fundamentals — sounds, everyday words, and confidence — so learners have a base they can keep building on.

Small, live classes

No more than 10 children per class, meeting live online on Saturdays and Sundays, so every child gets the attention they deserve.
