Online courses for children & teens

Joyful, play-based Yoruba.

We teach Yoruba to children and teens ages 5–17. Our focus is foundation-building — a confident, joyful start in the language — with classes grouped by age and level so every learner is with peers at the right stage.

How we teach

Learning that feels like play.

Play-based & joyful

Children learn through games, songs, stories, and conversation. When it's fun, they stay curious — and they keep coming back.

Foundation-building

We focus on strong fundamentals — sounds, everyday words, and confidence — so learners have a base they can keep building on.

Grouped by age & level

Classes are arranged by age and level, so each child learns alongside peers at a similar stage, at a pace that fits.
