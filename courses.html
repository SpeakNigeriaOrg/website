Online courses for children

Yoruba lessons that make the language click.

Our Yoruba courses are built for children — clear, playful, and paced so young learners stay engaged and keep coming back.

Yoruba Available now

Ages 3–6

First words

Songs, sounds, and simple words. Little ones meet the language through play, rhythm, and repetition.

Ages 7–11

Everyday language

Greetings, family, food, and conversation. Children build real vocabulary they can use at home.

Ages 12–17

Reading & culture

Deeper grammar, reading, and the stories and culture behind the words. For confident young speakers.

Igbo Building next

We're looking for a teacher to help lead Igbo.

Igbo is the next language we're building, and we want to build it with a teacher who leads — not just delivers lessons. If you love teaching Igbo to children, believe learning should be joyful and game-oriented, and want a leading role in shaping a curriculum from the ground up, we'd love to talk. These are early, open conversations — your ideas would genuinely help set the direction.

How it works

Simple to join, easy to keep up with.

  • Online and flexible. Lessons happen online, so children can join from anywhere.
  • Age-appropriate groups. Learners are grouped by age so the pace fits.
  • Supported by resources. Courses pair with our free games and videos for practice between lessons.
