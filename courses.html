Online courses for children

Live lessons that make the language click.

Our online courses are built for children — clear, playful, and paced so young learners stay engaged and keep coming back.

Ages 3–6

First words

Songs, sounds, and simple words. Little ones meet the language through play, rhythm, and repetition.

Ages 7–11

Everyday language

Greetings, family, food, and conversation. Children build real vocabulary they can use at home.

Ages 12–17

Reading & culture

Deeper grammar, reading, and the stories and culture behind the words. For confident young speakers.

How it works

Simple to join, easy to keep up with.

  • Online and flexible. Lessons happen online, so children can join from anywhere.
  • Age-appropriate groups. Learners are grouped by age so the pace fits.
  • Supported by resources. Every course pairs with our free games and videos for practice between lessons.
