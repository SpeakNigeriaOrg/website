About Speak Nigeria

Keeping heritage languages within reach.

We help children connect with Nigerian heritage languages — so the words, and everything they carry, pass on.

Our mission

Speak Nigeria is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to helping children learn and keep Nigerian heritage languages. We teach through online courses, and we build free, open resources that parents, teachers, and schools can use anywhere.

For many families, especially those raising children away from home, a heritage language can quietly slip away in a single generation. We want to change that. A language isn't only a way to speak — it carries family history, culture, and a sense of belonging. When a child holds on to the language, they hold on to all of it.

Where we are now

We're starting with Yoruba, where our courses and resources are already available. Igbo is next — we're in early conversations with teachers to help shape it. Over the longer term, we plan to grow into Hausa, Bini, Ijaw, and Efik too.

Alongside the courses, we're building a free, open-source learning game for Nigerian languages. Because it's open, it can grow in many languages at once — which is where volunteers and speakers make an enormous difference. If you'd like to be part of that, our Get Involved page is the place to start.

What we believe

Language is belonging

Words connect children to family, culture, and identity — not just vocabulary, but a place to stand.

Open by default

Our games, videos, and materials are free and open, so any family or classroom can use them.

Joyful learning

Children learn best when it feels like play. We build for curiosity, warmth, and fun.

Who we serve

We build for the whole circle around a child. Parents get simple tools to bring the language into everyday life at home. Teachers and schools get open lesson materials and games they can drop into a classroom. And children get lessons made just for them — clear, playful, and encouraging.

Get involved

Speak Nigeria grows through the people who care about this work. Whether you speak a Nigerian language, teach, build software, or just want to support what we do, there's a place for you. See how to get involved, or reach us any time at hello@speaknigeria.org.
