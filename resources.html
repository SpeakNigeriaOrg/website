Free & open

Open resources for Nigerian languages.

Everything here is free to use. We build in the open so any family or classroom can pick it up — no cost, no barriers — and so anyone who speaks a Nigerian language can help it grow.

Our flagship project In development

Open-source game

An open-source learning game for Nigerian languages.

We're building a Nigerian-language version of GCompris, a free, open-source learning suite for children. Because it's open-source and multi-platform, it can reach children on computers, tablets, and phones — and we'll be able to publish it on app stores. And because it's built to be translated, it can grow in many languages at once.

That last part is the exciting bit: with volunteers translating and recording words in their language, we can build for Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Bini, Ijaw, Efik, and beyond — all in parallel.

Why open

Free to use, free to build on.

We keep our games and materials open on purpose. Teachers can adapt them, developers can contribute, speakers can add their language, and families never hit a paywall. Heritage shouldn't be locked behind a price.
