Why we do this

Our languages carry memory, family, and belonging. When children keep the words, they keep the connection.

Many Nigerian children growing up abroad lose the thread of their heritage language within a generation. Speak Nigeria exists to make that thread easy to hold onto, through joyful learning and resources that don't cost families anything.

Speak a Nigerian language? Help us teach it.

We're building open-source learning games and we need volunteers to translate words and phrases and record them in their language — Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Bini, Ijaw, Efik, and more. You don't need to be a teacher. If you speak it, you can help.
