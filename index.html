Why we do this

A language carries memory, family, and belonging. When children keep the words, they keep the connection.

Many Nigerian children growing up abroad lose the thread of their heritage language within a generation. Speak Nigeria exists to make that thread easy to hold onto — through joyful teaching and resources that don't cost families anything.
